## ❓ **FAQs (Updated)**

### Q1: What is the Become Partner plan?
**A:** The Become Partner plan is a partnership program where you pay ₹1,499 as one-time processing fee to register. After registration, our company will discuss revenue sharing terms with you. The profit distribution will be decided based on mutual agreement for each deal.

### Q2: How does revenue sharing work?
**A:** After paying ₹1,499 processing fee, you become our partner. For each property deal, the revenue will be shared between you and the company based on mutual agreement. The exact percentage will be decided during partnership discussion.

### Q3: What types of properties can I list?
**A:** As a partner, you can list and manage multiple properties - PG/Hostels, Residential apartments/houses, Commercial spaces, or Plot/Land. Revenue sharing will be decided based on agreement for each property deal.

### Q4: Is the partnership fee recurring?
**A:** No. The ₹1,499 is a one-time processing fee to register as a partner. After registration, you'll earn revenue share from each deal as per mutual agreement. There are no monthly or recurring fees for partnership.
